# CSM Onboarding — Day 1 / Week 1 / Month 1

## Day 1 {#day-1}

- Read Parts 1–3 of the CSM SOP end to end — foundations, interaction, intake.
- Shadow **3 live calls** with a senior CSM.
- Tour the tools: ServiceTitan, phone system, LSA dashboard, Posh log — `sop.csm.tools`.
- Set up accounts and phone extension.
- **Sign the SOP acknowledgment.**

## Week 1 {#week-1}

- Read Parts 4–7 — booking, channels, post-booking, governance.
- Take **5 supervised calls**.
- First 5 solo bookings reviewed **same day**.
- Role-play every objection script — `script.objection.price`, `script.objection.quote-only`, `script.objection.callback-spouse`, `script.objection.diy`.
- **Emergency triage drill — 3 scenarios** against `protocol.emergency.triage`.

## Month 1 {#month-1}

- **20 solo bookings scored** against the rubric — `governance.qa.csm`.
- First 1:1 coaching review — `governance.coaching`.
- Certify on all 5 channel playbooks: `playbook.inbound`, `playbook.lsa`, `playbook.web`, `playbook.posh`, `playbook.sms`.
- Membership pitch rate ≥ 60% verified.
- **Signed off by the Manager.**

## Why the triage drill is a drill {#triage-drill}

Reading the emergency matrix is not the same as recognizing a gas smell buried in a rambling call while you're mid-intake. Run it live, unannounced, three times in week one. It is the only pass/fail item on the rubric, and the only one where getting it wrong hurts a customer rather than a number.

## Same-day review in week one {#same-day}

The first five solo bookings get reviewed the day they happen, not at the end of the week. Habits set in the first week are the ones that stick — including the wrong ones.

## Sign-off is a real gate {#sign-off}

Month 1 ends with a Manager sign-off, not automatically. A CSM who hasn't hit the bar stays in supervised handling until they do. Signing off early to fill a seat is how a bad habit becomes a team standard.
