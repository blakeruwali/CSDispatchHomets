# Channel Playbook — Customer Referrals

> **Not yet written.** This document is a known gap, not an oversight. `sop.csm.lead-source` already commits us to something this page has to define: *"Referring customers earn a credit only if we log the name."* That promise currently has no procedure behind it.

## What we already do {#current}

- Ask "how did you hear about us?" on every call — `sop.csm.lead-source`.
- Log referrals as **Referral — [name]**, with the referrer's name captured verbatim.

## What this doc must define {#needed}

Blocking questions for the owner — each one changes what a CSM says on the phone:

1. **What is the referral credit?** Dollar amount, account credit, free tune-up, or membership month? Nothing else on this page can be written until this is answered.
2. **Who gets it** — the referrer only, or both parties?
3. **When does it trigger** — at booking, at job completion, or at payment?
4. **How is it applied in ServiceTitan** — credit on the account, discount code, or manual adjustment?
5. **Does it expire?**
6. **Who tells the referrer?** Automated, or a CSM callback? Right now, nobody does — the referrer is thanked by the person they referred, if at all.
7. **Is there a cap** per customer per year?

## Interim handling {#interim}

Until this is written: **capture the referrer's name verbatim on every referral** so credits can be applied retroactively once the policy exists. Do not promise a credit or name an amount on the phone — we cannot yet honor a specific number.
