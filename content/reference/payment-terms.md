# Payment Terms — Cash and Financing

> **The deposit is the same either way: {{price:deposit_rule}}. What differs is when the rest is collected, and that difference exists to protect the equipment order.**

**Never improvise a payment schedule at the kitchen table.** The install date depends on equipment being ordered, and equipment is ordered against collected funds.

## Cash or check {#cash}

| Stage | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **{{price:deposit_rule}}** | At signing. **Write the receipt on the spot.** |
| 2 | **{{price:payment_stage_2_cash}}** | Before the install date |
| 3 | **{{price:payment_stage_3}}** | At completion, before the crew leaves |
| 4 | Final invoice + warranty documents | Emailed the same day |

**Why we prefer it, honestly:** we keep 100% of the sale price with no dealer fee, funding is immediate, the paperwork is simpler with no credit application to wait on, and a customer who writes a check very rarely cancels.

**How to present it:**

> "For cash or check, we just need {{price:deposit_rule}} to lock in your install date. Then {{price:payment_stage_2_cash}} before the install, and the balance when the job's done."
>
> "Most homeowners prefer this because it's straightforward — no interest, no monthly payments, just a clean deal."
>
> "I'll write you a receipt right now and we'll get you on the schedule."

## Financing {#financing}

| Stage | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **{{price:deposit_rule}}** | At signing. Receipt required — it secures the install date |
| 2 | **{{price:payment_stage_2_financing}}** | 3 days before install — secures equipment ordering |
| 3 | Lender funds the balance | Typically 3–5 business days after completion |

**The order of operations:**

1. Present Good / Better / Best, leading with the monthly on Better and Best
2. Run the credit application — **soft pull first**, on the tablet, about 60 seconds
3. Review the approval with the customer: **monthly payment, term, and APR** — all three, out loud
4. Customer e-signs the financing agreement
5. Collect the deposit and write the receipt
6. Schedule the install

**What to know about the economics:**

- **The dealer fee is real** — typically 8–15% depending on the promotion — and it is **factored into pricing**, not added at the table. There is no separate "cash price."
- **0% promotional options** are commonly 12–18 months same-as-cash on qualifying purchases.
- **Extended terms** (60–144 months) lower the monthly and raise the total cost. Say so if asked; do not volunteer a term that costs them more than they need.
- **Approval is not guaranteed.** Have a plan B ready: co-signer, a different lender, or a reduced scope.

**How to present it:**

> "A lot of our customers go with financing because it lets them get the best system now without a big hit to savings."
>
> "Let me run a quick pre-qualification — it's a soft pull, won't affect your credit score. Takes about 60 seconds."
>
> "You're approved. Here's what it looks like: $[amount] a month for [term] months."
>
> "We just need {{price:deposit_rule}} today to lock you in, and then three days before your install we collect the remaining balance so we can secure your equipment."
