# Install Pricing Framework

> ⚠️ **These are framework ranges for orientation, not quotes.** Always price from the estimating tool. **Never quote from memory** — the ranges below do not know about accessibility, code requirements, duct modifications, electrical upgrades or permit fees at a specific address.

Use this page to know roughly where a conversation is heading and which tier to build. Use the estimating tool for the number you say out loud.

## Residential {#residential}

### Gas furnace + AC {#furnace-ac}

| Tier | Range | Spec | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good | $8,500 – $11,500 | 80% AFUE / 14 SEER2 | Single-stage both, standard ductwork |
| Better | $12,500 – $17,000 | 96% AFUE / 17 SEER2 | Two-stage furnace, variable blower, smart thermostat |
| Best | $18,500 – $25,000+ | 98% AFUE / 20+ SEER2 | Modulating furnace, inverter AC, zoning, full IAQ |

### Heat pump {#heat-pump}

| Tier | Range | Spec | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good | $7,500 – $10,000 | 15 SEER2 / 8 HSPF2 | Single-stage, electric backup, basic thermostat |
| Better | $11,500 – $16,000 | 17 SEER2 / 9 HSPF2 | Two-stage, smart thermostat, dual fuel option |
| Best | $17,000 – $24,000+ | 20+ SEER2 / 10+ HSPF2 | Variable speed inverter, cold-climate rated, full IAQ |

### AC only {#ac}

| Tier | Range | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Good | — | Single-stage, basic thermostat, standard install |
| Better | $9,800 – $13,500 | Two-stage, smart thermostat, hard start kit, surge protector |
| Best | $15,000 – $20,500+ | Variable speed inverter, zoning, IAQ bundle, premium warranty |

### Mini split, per zone {#mini-split}

| Tier | Range | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Good | $4,000 – $5,700 | Single-zone wall mount, basic remote |
| Better | $6,300 – $8,500 | Single-zone, WiFi control, enhanced filtration |
| Best | $9,200 – $13,500+ | Multi-zone (2–3 heads), hyper-heating, smart integration |

### Boiler, hydronic {#boiler}

| Tier | Range | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Good | $6,800 – $10,000 | Standard cast iron, basic controls |
| Better | $11,500 – $17,000 | High-efficiency, modulating, indirect water heater option |
| Best | $18,500 – $28,500+ | Condensing boiler, smart controls, radiant-floor ready |

## Add-ons {#add-ons}

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Smart thermostat | — | Included in Better and Best |
| Hard start kit | — | Protects the compressor; included Better and above |
| Surge protector | — | Whole-unit protection |
| UV air purifier | $800 – $1,500 | Kills mould and bacteria at the coil |
| Media air cleaner | $600 – $1,200 | 4" or 5" whole-home filtration |
| Humidifier / dehumidifier | $800 – $1,800 | Whole-home comfort control |
| Zoning system, 2-zone | $2,000 – $3,500 | Dampers, panel, extra thermostat |
| Duct modification | $500 – $3,000+ | Per area — resize, add runs, seal |
| Electrical panel upgrade | $1,800 – $3,500 | When the panel cannot support the new equipment |
| **Permit and inspection** | — | **Always included. Never skipped.** |

## Light commercial {#commercial}

| Type | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rooftop unit | $9,200 – $40,000+ | Per unit; varies by tonnage and crane needs |
| Split system | $11,500 – $34,500+ | Depends on lineset length and electrical |
| VRF / multi-zone | $28,500 – $115,000+ | Complex design, engineering required |

**VRF and anything multi-location is not quoted at the site.** Capture the equipment list and hand it to the Operations Manager.

## What these ranges do not include {#caveats}

Factor every one of these before you price: complexity, accessibility, code requirements, duct modifications, electrical upgrades, permit fees, crane or lift, and disposal.
