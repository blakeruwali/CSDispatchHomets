# Governance

How the SOP itself is controlled, scored, and improved — as distinct from the operational content it governs.

- `doc-control.md` — where the master SOP lives, who edits it, how changes are versioned and announced.
- `qa-rubric-csm.md` — the CSM call-scoring rubric and its weights. Mirrors the in-app rubric at `/checklist`.
- `coaching-cadence.md` — weekly 1:1, monthly team meeting, quarterly SOP review.

Departmental QA rubrics (dispatch, sales, install) live here alongside the CSM one as they are written.
