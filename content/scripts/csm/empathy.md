# Acknowledgement & Empathy Lines

The one line that goes between the greeting and the first intake question. Procedure in `sop.csm.active-listening`.

## Standard acknowledgement {#standard}

> "That sounds frustrating — let's get you taken care of."

## Then paraphrase {#paraphrase}

> "So your boiler stopped making heat overnight — is that right?"

The paraphrase is not optional politeness. It proves you listened and it catches a misheard problem before it becomes a misdispatched truck.

## Situational variants {#variants}

| Situation | Line |
|---|---|
| No heat, cold weather | "That's the last thing you need in this weather — let's get someone out to you." |
| No AC, heat advisory | "In this heat that's miserable. Let's get you on the schedule today if we can." |
| Water leak | "Okay — let's get that stopped and get someone out to you." |
| Repeat issue / callback | "You shouldn't have to call twice about this. Let me get it sorted." |
| Angry customer | "I hear you, and I'd feel the same way." See `sop.csm.de-escalation`. |

## What not to say {#avoid}

- ❌ "I understand." — Empty on its own; name the actual thing.
- ❌ "Unfortunately..." — Leads with the bad news posture.
- ❌ "That's a common issue." — Minimizes their problem to make ours sound routine.
- ❌ Any acknowledgement that arrives *after* the first intake question. Order matters.
