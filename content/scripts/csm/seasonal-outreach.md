# Seasonal Outreach Scripts

Proactive calls to existing customers before peak season. The only outbound work that reliably flattens the demand curve — every tune-up booked in September is a call we're not scrambling to answer in January.

## Pre-winter — September/October {#pre-winter}

> "Hi [name], this is [your name] from Home+ Air and Heat. With winter right around the corner, I wanted to reach out and see if we can get your heating system tuned up before the cold hits. A fall tune-up helps prevent breakdowns and keeps your energy bills down. We have openings [timeframe]. Would you like to schedule?"

## Pre-summer — March/April {#pre-summer}

> "Hi [name], this is [your name] from Home+ Air and Heat. Summer's coming and now is the perfect time to make sure your AC is ready to go. We'd love to get you on the schedule for a spring tune-up — it helps catch any issues before the heat wave. Can I book that for you?"

## How to run a campaign {#campaign}

- **Start {{price:seasonal_outreach_lead_time}} before peak season.** Calling in the first cold snap is too late — that's when the phones are already full.
- **Call order: members first**, then recent customers, then dormant accounts. Members are owed the tune-ups they've already paid for; see `sop.csm.membership-retention`.
- **Name a specific benefit** — efficiency, warranty compliance, comfort. "Just checking in" books nothing.
- **No answer → voicemail plus a text.** Script in `script.csm.voicemail`.
- **Tag every call in ServiceTitan** with the campaign tag, so we can tell what the outreach actually produced.

## Book it on the call {#book-now}

The point of the call is a booked window, not a warm feeling. If they're interested, offer two windows the way you would on any inbound booking — `sop.csm.availability` — and read it back. "Call us when you're ready" is a call you'll be making again in three weeks.

## When they say no {#no}

Leave it clean and note the reason. Seasonal outreach is a long game — a customer who declines the fall tune-up is still a customer, and pushing costs us the January emergency call they would otherwise have given us.
