# Objection — "I'll try to fix it myself first"

## The response {#response}

> "Fair enough. If it doesn't work out, call us back — the offer stands. One thing: **if it's gas, refrigerant, or electrical, please don't DIY — call us.**"

## Why it works {#why}

Arguing with a DIYer loses them permanently. Agreeing keeps the door open, and most of these calls come back within a week.

The safety line is not a sales tactic — it's the part of this script that matters most. Gas, refrigerant, and electrical work injures people every winter, and a customer who gets hurt after calling us is a failure regardless of whether they booked.

## Say the safety line every time {#safety}

Even when it feels like it's undercutting the friendly close. Especially then. If their described symptom involves gas smell, burning smell, or sparking, this is no longer a DIY conversation — go straight to `protocol.emergency.triage`.

## Leave it warm {#warm}

- No pressure, no "well, when it breaks worse..."
- Confirm they have our number.
- Note the call in ServiceTitan with the symptom so the callback picks up where this left off.
