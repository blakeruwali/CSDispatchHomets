# Customer Profile Recognition

Identify who you are talking to before you quote anything. The profile changes the fee, the membership pitch, and who is allowed to authorize work.

## The four profiles {#profiles}

| Profile | Signal | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Homeowner** | Owns the address, decision-maker is on the line | Standard flow. Pitch Home+. |
| **Rental / Tenant** | Says "my landlord" or "the property manager" | Get owner/PM name **and authorization before dispatch**. Do NOT pitch Home+. |
| **Commercial** | Business name, business address, invoice terms | Business+ pitch. {{price:diagnostic_commercial}} diagnostic, {{price:labor_commercial_hourly}} labor. |
| **HOA / Property Mgmt** | Multi-unit, PM company name | Route to the designated PM account contact. Manager approves scope. |

## Why tenants are different {#tenants}

A tenant cannot authorize spend on someone else's building. Dispatching on a tenant's word alone produces the worst outcome we have: a completed job nobody will pay for and a landlord who feels billed behind their back.

Get the owner or PM on record — name, phone, and explicit approval — before the truck moves. If the tenant cannot produce that, book nothing and tell them plainly:

> "I can absolutely get someone out — I just need your landlord or property manager to okay it first, since it's their system. Can you give me their name and number, or have them call me?"

And never pitch a tenant a membership on a system they don't own.

## Why HOA/PM is manager-approved {#hoa}

Property-management accounts usually carry negotiated pricing, a named contact, and a scope ceiling. Booking one on the standard flow overwrites terms you can't see. Route it, don't improvise it.
