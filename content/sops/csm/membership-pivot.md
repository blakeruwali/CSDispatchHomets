# Membership Pivot (Home+)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Every non-member call is an enrollment chance. |
| **When it applies** | Non-member, non-emergency, **after the diagnostic fee is accepted**. |
| **Owner** | CSM initially; Technician on-site. |

## The pitch {#pitch}

> "One quick thing — most of our customers on this kind of call end up joining Home+. It's {{price:membership_home_plus_monthly}}, includes {{price:membership_home_plus_tuneups}} tune-ups, waives the diagnostic fee today, and gives you {{price:membership_home_plus_repair_discount}} off the repair. It usually pays for itself on the first visit. Want me to add it?"

The close is a direct question. "Want me to add it?" — not "let me know if you're interested."

## Timing is the whole thing {#timing}

Pitch **after** the fee is accepted, never before. Before, it sounds like an upsell to dodge a charge. After, it's the obvious arithmetic: the membership costs less than the diagnostic it waives.

## Rules by situation {#rules}

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Emergency call | **Skip.** Mention after the issue is resolved. |
| Already a member | Thank them and confirm perks. See `sop.csm.membership-status`. |
| Lapsed | Offer reinstatement at the same {{price:membership_home_plus_monthly}}. |
| Rental / tenant | **Do NOT pitch** — the owner enrolls. See `sop.csm.customer-profiles`. |
| Commercial | Pitch **Business+** instead. |

## Never pitch during an emergency {#emergency}

A customer standing outside their house because of a gas smell is not evaluating a maintenance plan. Pitching there reads as predatory and it is remembered. Resolve first, mention later, and only if the call ends calm.

## We measure the pitch, not the close {#measurement}

Target is ≥ 60% pitch rate on non-member calls — see `sop.csm.kpis`. You control whether it was offered; you do not control whether they said yes. Never skip the pitch because you assume the answer.
