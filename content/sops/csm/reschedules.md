# Reschedules & Cancellations

## The four steps {#steps}

1. **Ask why.** "Totally fine — what's changed?"
2. **Try to save the job first** — offer two alternate windows.
3. **Same-day cancel** → Dispatch approval + reason on the ticket.
4. **Confirm the new window** with the full read-back — `sop.csm.confirmation`.

## "Ask why" is not a challenge {#ask-why}

Say it warmly. The reason is what tells you whether this is a save, a genuine conflict, or the start of a complaint we need to catch. A cancellation with no recorded reason is a data point we permanently lose.

## Situation rules {#rules}

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| 3rd reschedule on the same job | Manager review — likely a bad-fit customer |
| "Found someone cheaper" | **Do NOT price-match.** Note the lead source loss. |
| "It resolved itself" | Note it, move on, no pressure |
| Cancel **+ complaint** | Manager — **do not close the file** |

## Cancel plus complaint is never closed by a CSM {#complaint}

A customer who cancels *and* complains is a retention problem and, often, a review risk. Route it to the Manager with the file open and the reason recorded. Closing it is how a fixable complaint becomes a one-star review nobody saw coming. See `sop.csm.de-escalation`.

## No price matching, even here {#no-match}

Losing a job to a cheaper quote is an acceptable outcome. Matching it teaches the customer our price is negotiable and invites the same conversation on every future job. Log the loss with the competitor name if they'll give it — that's genuinely useful.
