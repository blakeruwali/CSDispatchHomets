# CSM Role & Responsibilities

The CSM is the first and last human voice on most customer touchpoints. Own the call from ring to confirmation text.

## What the CSM owns {#owns}

- Answering inbound calls, web forms, LSA leads, and Posh handoffs.
- Complete intake — customer info, system, symptom, access.
- Booking to the right window with the right business unit.
- Diagnostic-fee framing and the Home+ membership pitch.
- Confirmation text and pre-visit expectations.
- Reschedules, cancellations, and no-show recovery.

## What the CSM does NOT own {#does-not-own}

| Not ours | Whose it is |
|---|---|
| Dispatch board and tech assignment | Dispatch |
| On-site pricing or scope changes | Technician |
| Fee waivers, refunds, complaint resolution | Manager |

Handing one of these off is not a failure — doing it *fast and cleanly* is the job. The customer should never feel the seam.

## Boundary rule {#boundary}

If a request falls in the "does NOT own" column, the CSM's job is to name the next step and own the handoff, not to improvise an answer:

> "That's a call my manager makes, and I want you to get the right answer — let me get them the details and have them reach you today."

Never say "that's not my department."
