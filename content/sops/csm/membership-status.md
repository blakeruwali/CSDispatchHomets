# Membership Status Check

**Check ServiceTitan before you quote anything.** Pitching a member the membership they already have loses trust faster than almost anything else we do on the phone.

## Status → action {#status}

| Status | Action |
|---|---|
| **Active member** | Thank them explicitly. Diagnostic {{price:membership_diagnostic_posture}}. Apply the {{price:membership_home_plus_repair_discount}} repair discount. |
| **Lapsed** | Offer reinstatement at the same rate — {{price:membership_home_plus_monthly}}. |
| **Non-member** | Standard flow. Pitch after the diagnostic fee is accepted — see `sop.csm.membership-pivot`. |

## Look it up before you open your mouth about price {#lookup}

The lookup takes seconds and it changes the entire call: an active member hears "your diagnostic is covered," not "our diagnostic is {{price:diagnostic_residential}}." Quoting a member full price and then walking it back makes us look like we tried.

## Thank members explicitly {#thank}

Don't just silently apply the benefit. Name it:

> "I see you're on Home+ — thank you for being a member. That means there's no diagnostic charge today, and you'll get {{price:membership_home_plus_repair_discount}} off whatever the repair comes to."

Membership renewal is won on calls like this one, not at renewal time.

## Lapsed is the warmest lead we have {#lapsed}

A lapsed member already bought the value once. Offer reinstatement at the same rate before the fee conversation, not after — it changes the fee conversation entirely.
