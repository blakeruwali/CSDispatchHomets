# Confirmation & Expectations Read-Back

Read back in **this order** at the end of every booking. No exceptions, no abbreviated version for repeat customers.

## The six-point read-back {#read-back}

1. **Name + address** — "123 Main Street, Massapequa, correct?"
2. **Window** — "Tech out between 8 and 10 tomorrow morning."
3. **Fee** — "{{price:diagnostic_residential}} diagnostic, credited toward the repair if approved."
4. **Tech name** (if assigned) — "You'll get a text with his photo before he heads out."
5. **Prep** — "Someone 18+ home, clear access to the [unit]."
6. **Confirm text** — "Sending confirmation now — hit Y to confirm."

## Why every item is on the list {#why}

Each one is a dispute we've already had:

- **Address** — the wrong-house truck roll.
- **Window** — "I thought you said afternoon."
- **Fee** — the doorstep argument. Saying the number twice on the call ends it.
- **Tech photo** — the customer who won't open the door for a stranger.
- **Prep** — the locked gate, the empty house, the minor home alone.
- **Confirm text** — the ticket that was never actually confirmed.

Thirty seconds of read-back prevents all six.

## Say the fee out loud even if they already agreed {#fee}

Especially then. The fee is the most-disputed item at the door, and "we discussed it earlier in the call" is not something a customer accepts standing in their driveway. Repeat it, plainly, and note on the ticket that it was confirmed.

## Send the text before you hang up {#text}

Not after the call, not at the end of the batch. Before you hang up, so you can say "you should see it come through now" and know it landed.
