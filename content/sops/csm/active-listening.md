# Active Listening & Acknowledgement

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | The customer feels heard before they're processed. |
| **When it applies** | After the greeting, before any intake question. |
| **Owner** | CSM |

## The four steps {#steps}

1. **Let them finish the first sentence uninterrupted.** No matter how obvious the problem is by word four.
2. **Acknowledge with one line.** "That sounds frustrating — let's get you taken care of."
3. **Paraphrase the issue back.** "So your boiler stopped making heat overnight — is that right?"
4. **Only then start data collection.**

## Why the order is fixed {#why}

Jumping to "can I get your address?" tells the customer they are a ticket. Thirty seconds of acknowledgement is the cheapest trust we ever buy, and it is the difference between a caller who accepts the diagnostic fee and one who calls the next company on the list.

The paraphrase does double duty: it proves you listened *and* it catches a misheard problem before it becomes a misdispatched truck.

## Scored behaviour {#scored}

QA scores this 0/1/2:

- **0** — jumped straight to the intake form.
- **1** — brief acknowledgement ("sorry to hear that") then questions.
- **2** — empathetic reflect-back naming the actual pain, then questions.
