# System Type Identification

**Ask, don't guess.** The system type routes the job to the right business unit and tells the tech what to put on the truck.

## The five questions {#questions}

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| "Is it forced air or radiators?" | Furnace vs. boiler |
| "Does it also cool in summer?" | Heat pump vs. furnace + AC |
| "Wall-mounted individual units per room?" | Mini-split |
| "Do you know the fuel — gas, oil, or electric?" | Correct business unit + tech skill |
| "Where is it located?" | Basement / attic / roof affects the gear the tech brings |

## Customers don't know the vocabulary {#vocabulary}

Most callers cannot name their system, and asking "do you have a boiler or a furnace?" produces a confident wrong answer more often than a right one. That is why every question above describes something the customer can *see or feel* rather than something they'd have to know.

If they genuinely don't know after all five, that's fine — note it, note the fuel and location, and let the tech identify it on site. An honest "unknown" is far better than a guess that sends the wrong truck.

## Location matters more than people expect {#location}

Roof and attic units change the job: ladders, fall protection, sometimes a second tech. A rooftop unit also means the job is commercial by definition — see `sop.csm.res-vs-comm`.
