# Project Closeout — Invoice, Warranty, Records

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | The paperwork behind the install is finished within five business days, while the facts are still fresh. |
| **When it applies** | Every completed install. |
| **Owner** | Project Manager. |

## Within five business days {#five-days}

| Task | Standard |
|---|---|
| Final invoice | Matches the signed agreement plus any signed change orders. No surprises, no rounding. |
| Balance collected | Per `sop.sales.paperwork`. An open balance over 10 days is escalated to the owner. |
| Equipment records | Every installed unit attached to the customer's location with model and serial — `sop.field.equipment-capture` |
| Manufacturer warranty | Registered with serials. Unregistered equipment is a warranty claim we will lose in year six. |
| Rebates and utility filings | Submitted, with the reference number on the job |
| Permit / inspection | Closed or scheduled — `sop.projects.permits` |
| Membership | Enrolled where sold, with the first maintenance visit already on the schedule |
| Comfort check | Booked at 10–14 days — `sop.projects.comfort-check` |

## What the customer receives {#customer-packet}

Emailed, in one message, within five business days: the paid invoice, the equipment list with model and serial numbers, warranty terms and registration confirmation, permit/inspection status, their membership details if enrolled, and the comfort check date.

That packet is what they will hand a buyer's inspector in eight years, and it is why they will call us instead of a search result.

## A project is not closed until {#definition-of-done}

All of: balance settled or on an agreed plan, warranty registered, equipment recorded, permit resolved, comfort check booked. Anything short of that stays open and stays on the Project Manager's board.
