# The CS Team's Review Ask

The technician owns the on-site moment. The CS team owns everything the moment missed — the customer the tech couldn't ask, the customer who said "later," and the customer whose delight showed up on the phone instead of the driveway.

## When the CS team asks {#when}

A CSM asks for a review when:

- The job closed with **no tech ask** — the tech flagged it, the customer was rushed, or the moment just wasn't there on site.
- The **follow-up call** surfaces a happy customer — `sop.csm.post-service-followup` ends in the review ask whenever satisfaction is clearly expressed.
- The customer **volunteers praise on any call** — booking, rescheduling, warranty, anything. "Your tech was wonderful" is a review ask waiting to be answered.

The ask itself, word for word, is `script.csm.review-request` — send the link while they're still on the phone.

## One ask per customer {#one-ask}

Before asking, check whether the automated request already went out — most review requests fire automatically two days post-service for survey scores of {{price:review_survey_gate}} or higher (`sop.csm.confirmation-cadence`).

- Automation already sent + customer ignored it → **no manual ask.** Two requests for one job reads as desperate.
- Automation sent + customer brings it up happily on the phone → help them do it right there: "The link in that text is the fastest way — it takes about 30 seconds."
- Automation never fired (low score, missing survey) → the manual ask is the only chance. Take it if they're happy; skip it entirely if they're not.

## Never ask an unhappy customer {#never}

Obvious, and it still happens when working down a call list. If the follow-up call reveals a problem, the review ask is off the table for that call — full stop. Solve the problem, log it, and let the remedy earn the review another day.

## Why this side matters {#why}

The tech side of the program has a single point of failure: one busy afternoon, one awkward goodbye, and the ask never happened. The CS team is the net underneath it — which is why review requests are part of this team's job, not a favor to it. Good reviews bring the next callers; the CS team talks to them first.
