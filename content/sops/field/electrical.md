# Complaint: Breaker Tripping or Short Cycling

**Not yet written.**

Repeated breaker trips, short cycling, and the amp-draw work that separates a nuisance trip from a compressor on its way out.

Until this exists, work the call under the general standard — measure the cause, prove it with a number, and end with a written price (`sop.field.diagnostics`). Thresholds are in `reference.diagnostic-readings`.

This placeholder is deliberate. A gap we have named is a gap somebody can close; a gap nobody wrote down is one that gets discovered by a customer.
