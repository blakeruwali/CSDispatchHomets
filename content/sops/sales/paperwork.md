# Paperwork and Next Steps

> **All of it happens before you leave the driveway. A sale that is not written up is not a sale, and the customer's enthusiasm is at its highest point right now.**

## The checklist {#checklist}

Work it in order. Every item happens on this visit.

| # | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Complete the contract in ServiceTitan** | All three options documented, chosen option highlighted — so the record shows they were offered a choice |
| 2 | **Collect the signature**, digital preferred | *"Let me get your approval right here"* |
| 3 | **Collect the deposit** — {{price:deposit_rule}} | Receipt written on the spot. Terms in `reference.payment-terms` |
| 4 | **Schedule the installation date** | Lock it in now. Never leave it open |
| 5 | **Confirm permit requirements** | Tell them whether a permit is needed and what to expect |
| 6 | **Set expectations for install day** | Duration, crew size, what to move or clear, where to park |
| 7 | **Leave your card and materials** | *"If you think of any questions, call me directly"* |

**All three options stay on the record**, not just the one they took. It is how a manager can see what was offered, and it is what protects you if a customer later says they were only shown the expensive one.

## Setting install-day expectations {#expectations}

Two minutes here saves a phone call and a bad review:

- **How long** the install takes, honestly — including the possibility of a second day.
- **How many people** will be in the house.
- **What they need to clear** — the path to the equipment, the attic hatch, the driveway.
- **Whether the power or heat goes off**, and for how long.
- **That a permit inspection may follow** on a separate day.

## Payment stages {#payment}

Deposit, staged payments and their timing differ between cash and financing and are set out in one place: `reference.payment-terms`. **Do not improvise a payment schedule at the table** — the equipment order depends on it.

## If it is not in ServiceTitan {#servicetitan}

It did not happen (`sop.csm.tools`). A verbal agreement, a photo of a handwritten figure, or a promise to "write it up tomorrow" produces a customer expecting one thing and a company delivering another.
