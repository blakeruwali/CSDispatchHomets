# Arrival

> **The estimate starts before the door opens. What you do in the fifteen minutes before you knock decides how the next hour goes.**

## Before you arrive {#before}

- **Call or text 15 minutes out.** *"Hi, this is [Name] from Homets. I'm about 15 minutes away."*
- **Read the job notes and the customer history in ServiceTitan.** What the CSM captured, what we have done here before, what equipment is on file (`sop.field.equipment-capture`).
- **Check membership status** — it changes the conversation and it is embarrassing to get wrong (`sop.field.membership`).
- **Clean up your truck.** They will look at it.

## At the door {#door}

- **Boot covers on before they open the door.** Not after — before.
- Smile, introduce yourself by name, hand them your card.
- *"Hi, I'm [Name], your comfort advisor from Homets."*

## Set the stage {#stage}

Tell them what is about to happen, so nothing in the next hour is a surprise:

> "I'm going to do a thorough evaluation of your system. It'll take about 30–45 minutes to check everything. Then I'll sit down with you and go over all your options."

Then ask: *"Is there anything specific that prompted this?"* — and let them answer at length.

**Ask the decision question now, not at the end:**

> "Is there anyone else involved in making this decision? I want to make sure everyone has the information they need."

If a spouse or partner is out, offer to come back when both are home. Asking this at the start prevents the most common stall there is, and asking it at the end sounds like an accusation (`script.objection.sales` §4).

## Build rapport, honestly {#rapport}

- Notice the home and compliment something **genuine**. A false compliment is worse than none.
- Ask about the family — pets, kids, which rooms they actually use.
- Listen for comfort complaints. Take mental notes; you will use them in the presentation.

## Never {#never}

- **Never jump straight to the equipment.** Walking past the customer to the basement tells them the system matters and they do not.
- Never open with price.
- Never make a promise on the doorstep that the evaluation has not earned yet.
