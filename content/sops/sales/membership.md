# Membership in the Package

> **On Better and Best it is already included. It is not an add-on to ask about — it is a feature to explain.**

## Do not ask, tell {#tell}

Never *"Do you want to add the membership?"* — that invites a no about something they already have. It is part of the package:

> "This package comes with your Home+ membership, so you're covered from day one."

## What to say {#script}

> "The Better and Best packages both include your first year of Home+ membership."
>
> "That gets you {{price:membership_home_plus_tuneups}} tune-ups a year — one for heating, one for cooling — so your new system stays under warranty and running at peak efficiency."
>
> "You also get {{price:membership_home_plus_repair_discount}} off any future repairs, and priority scheduling."
>
> "Most homeowners keep it going after the first year because the tune-ups alone are worth more than the membership."
>
> "Think of it like an insurance policy for your brand new investment."

## What it is worth, honestly {#value}

| Benefit | Why it matters to them |
|---|---|
| {{price:membership_home_plus_tuneups}} tune-ups a year | Keeps the manufacturer warranty valid |
| {{price:membership_home_plus_repair_discount}} off repairs | Ongoing savings |
| Priority scheduling | Skip the line in a heatwave |
| Extended labor warranty | Peace of mind on a new system |
| Transferable to a new owner | Adds value at resale |

The warranty point is the strongest one and the least used. **Most manufacturers require documented annual maintenance for the warranty to stay valid** — a membership is how that documentation happens automatically.

> ⚠️ **Never sell membership as a way to avoid an after-hours or emergency surcharge.** There is no such surcharge, for anyone, member or not (`reference.guarantees`). Presenting a benefit everybody already has is the fastest way to make a customer doubt everything else you told them — and they will find out, because the no-surcharge promise is on our website.
