# Financing

> **Ask about monthly before you say the total. Once the frame is monthly, a $15,000 decision becomes a $189 decision — and for most households that is genuinely the decision they are making.**

## The sequence {#sequence}

**1. Ask permission first.**

> "Before I share the investment, let me ask — would it help if I could show you monthly payment options?"

Most say yes. Now everything that follows is framed monthly, and you were invited to do it.

**2. Anchor the monthly number.**

> "The Better package — which is what I'd recommend — comes out to about $X/month with approved credit."

**3. Offer same-as-cash where it applies.**

> "We also offer 0% for 18 months — so if you pay it off in that window, zero interest. A lot of homeowners use that."

**4. Close on the choice, not on yes-or-no.**

> "Would you like to go with the monthly option, or did you want to do the full investment today?"

Both answers are a yes. That is legitimate here because both really are available — an either/or close is only honest when both branches exist.

## Comparisons {#comparisons}

Comparing a monthly payment to an everyday expense is fair when the comparison is real and not belittling. *"Less than most people's streaming subscriptions combined"* is fine. Comparing it to something that implies they waste money is not — you are talking to someone about their household budget, and condescension ends the sale on the spot.

## Never say {#never}

- **"Do you want to finance?"** — sounds like charity. Ask if they want to *see monthly options*.
- **"Can you afford…?"**
- **"Your credit might not…"**

Nobody has to justify their finances to a technician standing in their kitchen.

## Running the application {#application}

- **Soft pull first**, and say so: *"It's a soft pull, won't affect your credit score. Takes about 60 seconds."*
- Review the approval out loud — **monthly payment, term, and APR**. All three. A customer who later discovers the APR from paperwork they signed in their kitchen is a customer we have lost permanently.
- Customer e-signs on the spot.

**Approval is not guaranteed.** Have a plan B ready before you need it — co-signer, a different lender, or a reduced scope. A declined application in front of a spouse is a humiliating moment, and how you handle the next thirty seconds decides whether they ever call us again. Move on briskly and matter-of-factly.

## What financing costs us {#dealer-fee}

Financing carries a dealer fee, and it is **factored into pricing** rather than added at the table. Nobody quotes a different price for cash. See `reference.payment-terms`.
