# techeo.com — Start Here

The website plan is thirteen documents and roughly 217,000 words. **Nobody should read it front to back.** It is written so each person reads their part, and so every question a builder, designer, writer, or lawyer would ask already has an answer.

## If you are the founder

Read, in this order:

1. **`00-website-brief.md`** — every decision in one place. Twenty minutes.
2. **`01-strategy.md`**, the top section only — what the site is for and how we'll know it works.
3. **`11-build-and-launch.md` #budget and #hours** — what it costs and whether it fits your week.
4. **`03-pages-v1.md` #home-top** — the homepage copy you will be approving.

Then make the decisions below. Everything else in the set carries a recommended default, so you only need to weigh in where you disagree.

### Things this plan corrected in the company plan

- **The website isn't free.** `techeo.financials` budgeted it at $0, assuming you'd build it yourself. The Phase 1 hours cap rules that out. See the correction note in `06-financial-model.md`.
- **LSA lead disputes no longer exist.** Google replaced them with automated credit reviews in 2024. The lever now is rating bad leads in the Leads tab. Fixed in `techeo.delivery` and `techeo.audit`. **`content/playbooks/lsa.md`, the Homets playbook, still describes disputes and should be reviewed.**
- **70% is Homets' standard, not a published result** — until the baseline shows it. Fixed in `techeo.positioning`.
- **Pilot audit hours.** `11` #hours shows Weeks 5, 6, and 8 running slightly over the Phase 1 cap, and `techeo.plan90`'s Weeks 4 and 7 already exceed it on the audit's own hour estimates. `11` open question 1 recommends pre-approving the small overages. The larger fix — spreading the first Homets audit across two weeks — is `techeo.plan90`'s to make.

## If you are the builder

Start with `00` (the brief), then `06` (architecture), `02` (URLs and navigation), `05` (design system), and `03` (every v1 string). `08` defines every form field and flow; `09` the analytics events; `07` the structured data, robots.txt, and llms.txt. `11` has your schedule, the QA checklist, and the launch-day runbook. **Check brief D16 first** — under lean v1, several `06` systems are deferred until their triggers.

## If you are counsel

`10-compliance.md` is written for you: a requirements checklist with the questions to answer, not legal advice. `10` #counsel-package lists what to send and when. The deadline that matters is **Mon Nov 16, 2026**, for sign-off on the consent texts and the analytics posture.

## If you are writing copy

`03` (v1) and `04` (v1.1–v3) hold the copy. `12` holds the editorial standards, the three quiet-period article briefs, and the case-study process. Brief D7 (numbers) and D8 (voice and banned words) apply to every sentence.

## How this plan was made

Drafted section by section against `00-website-brief.md`. Each section then went through two independent reviews — one for fidelity to the company plan, one for factual, legal, and platform accuracy — and was revised. After that came two rounds of cross-document consistency checking and a completeness review, then a final pass that resolved the remaining conflicts by hand. Claims about third-party platforms (Google, Stripe, Cloudflare, state and federal law) are marked "verify" wherever they may have changed. Check those before relying on them.
