# Positioning, Framework & Ideal Client

**Every agency in this industry sells traffic. Techeo sells booked jobs, and starts by fixing the ones the client already paid for and lost.**

## The one sentence {#pitch}

> Techeo is run by an HVAC operator. We install the same phone-and-follow-up system we run at Homets — written down, scored weekly, and held to a 70% booking-rate standard on qualified calls — then we scale the demand on top of it. And you keep every account.

**70% is our standard, not yet a published result.** It is the target in `content/sops/csm/kpis.md`. Once the Week 1 baseline is pulled, say what Homets actually books — and only claim "70%" as a result if the measured figure supports it.

Three claims, each load-bearing:

1. **Run by an operator.** Not a differentiator you can claim twice. Homets is the proof.
2. **The system is real and written down.** 51 published CSM documents, SLAs, a call-scoring rubric, channel playbooks. Competitors have slide decks.
3. **You keep every account.** The industry's trust wound. Naming it first wins the room.

## The acronym problem, and the fix {#framework}

The original stack was Marketing Engine Optimization over SEO, AEO, Sales Engine Optimization and Ads Engine Optimization. It has a defect worth fixing before a logo exists:

- **Sales** Engine Optimization abbreviates to **SEO** — already taken by Search.
- **Ads** Engine Optimization abbreviates to **AEO** — already taken by Answer.

A brand built on four initialisms where two collide will spend forever explaining itself. Do not invent replacements either: `PEO` is a Professional Employer Organization, which this exact audience uses for payroll, and `GEO` already means Generative Engine Optimization in the trade press.

**The fix: keep the two acronyms the market already knows, and name the other two in plain English.**

| Engine | Name we use | Acronym | What it governs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search | Search Engine Optimization | **SEO** | Local pack, GBP, service-area pages, technical crawl |
| Answer | Answer Engine Optimization | **AEO** | Citation inside AI assistants and AI search results |
| Ads | **the Ads Engine** | — | LSA, Google Ads, marketplace spend, budget pacing |
| Booking | **the Booking Engine** | — | Answer rate, speed to lead, booking rate, membership attach |

All four sit under **MEO — Marketing Engine Optimization**, the umbrella and the parent product. Techeo runs four engines. Two have acronyms because buyers already use them; two have names because inventing a third and fourth acronym costs more than it earns.

## Why Booking is the wedge {#wedge}

Lead with the Booking Engine, not Search. Four reasons, in order of how much they matter:

1. **Nobody else can sell it.** It requires having run a CSM desk. Our competitors' founders have not.
2. **It pays back in 30 days, not six months.** SEO earns trust slowly; a booking-rate lift shows up in the next monthly close.
3. **It costs the client nothing in new spend.** The money is already committed. We recover it. That is the easiest yes in the industry.
4. **It earns the right to the rest.** A client who watched us lift booking rate hands over the ad accounts without a fight. Retainer revenue lives in the Ads and Search engines; Booking is how we get there.

Search, Answer and Ads are the business. Booking is the door.

## Ideal client {#icp}

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| Trade | Residential HVAC, residential plumbing, or both |
| Revenue | $1.5M – $15M |
| Trucks | 3 – 25 |
| CRM | **ServiceTitan** — a hard qualifier, see below |
| Demand spend | Already buying leads: LSA, Angi, Thumbtack, Yelp, or Google Ads |
| Decision maker | Owner-operator still signing the checks |
| Geography | Outside Homets' market — see `07-homets-guardrails.md` |

**Why ServiceTitan is a hard qualifier, not a preference.** It is where our unfair advantage compounds: we read their data natively, we can wire booked-revenue back into ad platforms as offline conversions, and our SOPs already reference ST objects. A non-ST client doubles delivery cost and halves the moat. Decline them for the first year, politely, and keep a waiting list.

**Why the revenue band.** Under $1.5M there is no budget and no lead flow to recover. Over $15M there is an in-house marketing hire or an agency of record, a procurement process, and a sales cycle measured in quarters. The band is where the owner can say yes in one meeting and the spend is big enough to be worth fixing.

## Who we decline {#anti-icp}

Write this down so it is a policy and not a mood:

- Anyone inside Homets' service area. No exceptions, ever.
- New businesses with no existing lead flow. There is nothing to optimize.
- Anyone whose primary ask is a website redesign, a logo, or social media content.
- Anyone who wants us to hold their ad accounts.
- Price shoppers comparing us to a $900/mo SEO vendor. Different product; refer them out.
- Anyone unwilling to let us mystery-shop their phones. That refusal is the diagnosis.

## What Techeo does not sell {#not-sold}

Brand identity work. Social media content calendars. Generic web design. Print. Vehicle wraps. Search-only retainers with no conversion mandate — because a search-only engagement makes us responsible for traffic we cannot convert, which is precisely the trap the rest of the industry is in.

When asked for any of these: refer to a partner and take no fee for the referral in year one. Free referrals build the vendor network described in `05-go-to-market.md`, and that network is a lead source.

## The competitive read {#competition}

| Competitor type | Their strength | Our wedge |
|---|---|---|
| National trades marketing agencies | Scale, case studies, brand recognition | They cannot fix the phone. They sell leads and blame the client's staff when those leads do not book. |
| Local generalist agencies | Relationships, price | No trade expertise, no ST integration, no seasonality model. |
| Franchise / buying-group marketing programs | Bundled, trusted, cheap | Templated and lowest-common-denominator; no per-client conversion work. |
| In-house marketing coordinator | Cheap, loyal, always available | One person cannot cover four engines. Position as their force multiplier, not their replacement. |
| Doing nothing | Free | The Gap Statement in `03-engine-audit.md` exists entirely to make this the expensive option. |

The honest weakness to prepare for: **we have one case study and it is our own company.** Every prospect will note it. The answer is not to dodge it — it is the design partner program in `05-go-to-market.md`, which buys three more case studies inside six months, and the audit in `03`, which proves competence on *their* numbers rather than asking them to trust ours.
