# The First 90 Days — Week by Week

**Anchored to real dates, and deliberately not "90 days from today."** Counting forward from late September would put the selling phase in December and January — heating peak plus the holidays, the worst sales window in this industry and the worst month to be distracted from Homets. So the calendar is restructured around the seasonality table in `07-homets-guardrails.md`:

| Phase | Window | What happens | Techeo hours/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Build** | Sep 28 – Nov 27, 2026 | Everything that can be built without a client | 10–12 |
| **2 — Quiet** | Nov 30, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 | Legal, admin, content, relationships. No delivery commitments. | 3–5 |
| **3 — Sell** | Feb 1 – Apr 30, 2027 | Three design partners, staggered onboarding | 14–18 |

Phase 1 is where the detail lives, because it is the part that is fully within your control and roughly 85% of it is work Homets needs anyway.

**The baseline pull is the highest-value task of the whole 90 days.** It converts every claim in the plan from plausible to evidenced, and it is the entire content of the first case study.

**Phase 1 exit criteria.** All of the following, or Phase 3 slips rather than starting half-ready:

- [ ] A priced, tested Engine Audit that has been run three times (Homets + two pilots)
- [ ] A client SOP starter set that forks in under four hours
- [ ] LLC, bank, insurance, MSA, and the IP license in place
- [ ] A live one-page site
- [ ] 20 named targets and 10 referral sources
- [ ] The two blanks in `07` filled in
- [ ] Homets KPIs still green — the whole phase is void if this one fails

## Phase 2 — Quiet (Nov 30 – Jan 31) {#phase-2}

Heating peak and the holidays. Techeo does not sell, onboard, or deliver. **4 hours a week, capped**, spent only on:

- Closing every open item in `08`
- Trademark filing
- Bookkeeping and the first monthly close routine
- Three published pieces using the Homets numbers — the operator angle from `05`
- Warming the 10 referral sources with no ask attached
- Nothing else

The discipline of doing almost nothing for two months is the plan working as designed, not the plan stalling. Homets gets its peak season undivided.

## Phase 3 — Sell (Feb 1 – Apr 30) {#phase-3}

| Month | Target |
|---|---|
| February | 5 teardowns per week. 8+ discovery calls. First design partner signed. |
| March | Second partner signed. First partner onboarded. 2 paid audits sold. |
| April | Third partner signed. Second partner onboarded. Pipeline built for September. |

**Stagger the onboarding — one per month, never three at once.** Onboarding is the most founder-intensive phase of any engagement, and three simultaneous week-1s in the run-up to cooling season is precisely the failure the guardrails exist to prevent.

**Exit criteria, Apr 30:** three design partners signed, two onboarded, two paid audits delivered, the Homets case study published with real numbers, Homets KPIs green, founder hours under the 26/week cap. The third partner onboards in September — May–August is delivery only, with no new business and no onboarding, per the seasonality calendar in `07`. September reopens selling with three case studies in hand: Homets, plus the first two partners at their day-90 reviews (late June and late July).

## What is deliberately not in this plan {#not-in-plan}

No logo or brand identity project. No multi-page website. No paid advertising for Techeo. No hiring. No trade show. No CRM purchase. No fourth client. No content calendar.

Every one of them is a reasonable thing for this company to do eventually, and every one of them is a way to feel productive in Phase 1 without building the audit — which is the only thing in this document that actually has to exist.
