## The three decisions the plan rests on

1. **Lead with the Booking Engine, not Search.** It is the only product a competitor cannot copy, it pays back in 30 days, and it costs the client no new spend. `01` `#wedge`
2. **The audit is the company.** Build it before the website, before the brand, before anything. It qualifies, proves competence, prices the gap, and pays for itself. `03`
3. **No client #4 until someone else runs Homets' day.** Funded by Techeo revenue. The first hire is at Homets, not at Techeo. `07` `#gate`

## The one-paragraph version

Techeo sells the conversion system Homets already built, to HVAC and plumbing contractors outside Homets' market, starting with a $1,500 diagnostic that prices the revenue they are currently losing on the phone. The wedge is booking rate because no competitor can deliver it; the revenue is in ads and search, which the wedge earns the right to sell. It breaks even at one client and is capped at three until a Homets operations owner is in seat. Phase 1 is nine weeks of building the audit — work that is roughly 85% identical to finishing the Homets SOP and therefore worth doing either way.
